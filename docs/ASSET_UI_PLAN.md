# Asset and UI production plan

The visual target is a warm, readable, tactile mobile management game with an
original identity. References inform clarity and pacing; artwork, icons and
fictional brands remain original to Indie Empire.

## M3 phone-first screen priority

Visual hierarchy and one-handed clarity take priority over decorative art.
Polish these screens in this order: Studio, Staff, Employee Detail, Hiring,
Project Team, Office, and Finances. Each pass must establish a clear primary
action, scannable status hierarchy, compact/tall safe-area behavior, and 48 px
minimum touch targets before adding ornamental illustration.

## Acceptance rules

- Touch targets remain at least 48 px; primary actions remain 62 px.
- Icons remain legible at 18–24 px and never replace essential labels alone.
- Employee portraits share the skin, hair and clothing family of their physical office sprite.
- Every data visualization retains a readable textual equivalent.
- New assets load from stable IDs and survive save migrations.
